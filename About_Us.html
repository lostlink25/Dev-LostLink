About LostLink

Reuniting travelers with their lost belongings through innovative technology and dedicated support, making airport lost and found effortless.

Report a Lost Item

Our Mission

At LostLink, we aim to transform the lost and found experience by providing a seamless, user-friendly platform that connects travelers with their belongings. Our mission is to reduce the stress of losing items at airports by offering real-time tracking, secure claiming, and 24/7 support, setting a global standard for excellence in recovery services.

Our Services

Report Lost Items

Quickly submit detailed descriptions and images to initiate the search for your lost belongings.

Track in Real Time

Monitor the status of your lost item with instant updates on its location and recovery progress.

Secure Claiming

Verify and claim your found items through a safe, streamlined process.

Staff Tools

Empower airport staff with efficient tools to manage and match lost and found items.

Our Team

LostLink is powered by a dedicated team of travel industry professionals, technology innovators, and customer service experts. From developing cutting-edge tracking systems to partnering with airports worldwide, our team is committed to ensuring every traveler’s belongings are safely returned. With decades of combined experience, we strive to make your journey worry-free.

Our Achievements

Since launching, LostLink has reunited thousands of travelers with their lost items across major U.S. airports, including LAX, JFK, and ATL. We’ve partnered with over 50 airports globally, achieving a 90% recovery rate for reported items. Our platform has been recognized for its innovative approach to lost and found services, earning accolades from travel industry leaders.
